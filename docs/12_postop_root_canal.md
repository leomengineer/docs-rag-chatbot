# Post-Op Care: Root Canal

It is normal for the tooth to feel tender for a few days, especially when biting.

- Avoid chewing on that side until the permanent restoration (usually a crown) is placed.
- Take ibuprofen or acetaminophen as directed for discomfort.
- Sensitivity to pressure can last 1–2 weeks; sharp lingering pain or swelling should be reported.
- Keep the temporary filling intact; if it falls out, call us for a replacement.

If you develop facial swelling, fever, or pain that worsens after day 3, contact the office promptly.
