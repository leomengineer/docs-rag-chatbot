# Teeth Whitening Policy

We offer two whitening options:

1. **In-office whitening** — about 60–90 minutes, visible results the same day.
2. **Custom take-home trays** — impressions today, trays delivered in about 1 week, wear per instructions for 1–2 weeks.

**Not recommended if:** you are pregnant, have untreated cavities or active gum disease, or have severe enamel erosion. Whitening does not change the color of crowns, veneers, or fillings.

**Sensitivity:** Temporary cold sensitivity is common for 24–48 hours. Using a sensitivity toothpaste before and after helps.

**Maintenance:** Avoid staining foods/drinks (coffee, red wine, berries) for 48 hours after in-office whitening. Touch-up gel is available for purchase for take-home tray users.
