# Payment Plans and Financing

For treatment plans over $500, options include:

1. **Pay-in-full discount** — 5% off the cash portion when paid the day of treatment.
2. **In-house split** — 50% at start, 50% at completion for multi-visit treatment (credit card on file required).
3. **CareCredit** — third-party financing; approval and terms are set by CareCredit, not the clinic.
4. **Insurance assignment** — we bill your plan first; you pay the estimated patient portion at each visit. Balances after insurance posts are your responsibility.

We do not offer open-ended monthly in-house financing beyond the split option above.
