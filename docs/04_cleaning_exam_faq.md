# Cleaning and Exam FAQ

**How often should I get a cleaning?**  
Most patients should visit every 6 months. Patients with gum disease may need cleanings every 3–4 months.

**What happens at a routine visit?**  
A hygienist cleans your teeth, takes X-rays if due, and a dentist performs an exam. The visit usually lasts 45–60 minutes.

**Do you offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas)?**  
Yes, for anxious patients during restorative or surgical procedures. It is not typically used for routine cleanings.

**Can I bring my child?**  
Yes. We see children age 3 and up. For younger children we recommend a pediatric specialist and can provide referrals.
