# Post-Op Care: Extractions

**First 24 hours**

- Bite on gauze for 30–45 minutes; replace if soaking through.
- Do not spit, rinse vigorously, use straws, or smoke for 48 hours (risk of dry socket).
- Apply an ice pack to the cheek 15 minutes on / 15 minutes off.
- Soft foods only: yogurt, mashed potatoes, scrambled eggs, cool soups.
- Take prescribed pain medication as directed; ibuprofen is often preferred if you can take NSAIDs.

**Days 2–7**

- Begin gentle saltwater rinses (1/2 tsp salt in a cup of warm water) after meals.
- Brush carefully, avoiding the socket.
- Swelling usually peaks at 48–72 hours.

**Call us if:** bleeding that will not slow with pressure, fever over 101°F, increasing swelling after day 3, foul taste with severe pain (possible dry socket), or difficulty breathing/swallowing.
