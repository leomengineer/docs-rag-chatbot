# Booking and Cancellation Policy

## Scheduling

- Book by phone at (951) 555-0142 or through our online portal.
- New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete forms.
- Please bring a photo ID, insurance card, and a list of current medications.

## Cancellations

- Cancel or reschedule at least **24 hours** before your appointment.
- Late cancellations (under 24 hours) and no-shows may be charged a **$50 fee**.
- Repeated no-shows (2 or more in 12 months) may require a deposit to rebook.

## Late arrivals

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten or reschedule your visit so other patients stay on time.
