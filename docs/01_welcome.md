# Welcome to BrightSmile Dental Clinic

BrightSmile Dental Clinic is a family dental practice serving patients of all ages. We focus on preventive care, clear communication, and comfortable visits.

Our team includes general dentists, a hygienist, and a part-time oral surgeon for extractions and implant placement. We accept most major dental insurance plans and offer flexible payment options for uninsured patients.

Whether you need a routine cleaning, a second opinion on a treatment plan, or emergency care for a toothache, we are here to help.
