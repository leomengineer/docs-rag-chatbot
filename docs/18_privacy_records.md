# Privacy and Records (HIPAA Summary)

BrightSmile Dental Clinic protects your health information under HIPAA.

- We use your information to provide care, bill insurance, and operate the practice.
- We do not sell patient lists.
- You may request a copy of your records; please allow up to 15 business days. A reasonable copying fee may apply for paper records.
- To authorize release of records to another provider or a third party, complete our Records Release form.
- Questions about privacy: ask for the Privacy Officer at the front desk or email privacy at brightsmile.example.com.
