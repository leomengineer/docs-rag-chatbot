# Pre-Op Instructions: Root Canal

A root canal removes infected or inflamed pulp from inside the tooth, then seals the canals.

Before your appointment:

- Eat normally unless sedation is planned.
- Take prescribed antibiotics if they were started ahead of time — finish the course.
- Continue regular pain medication as needed; do not arrive in severe unmanaged pain without calling first.
- Plan 60–90 minutes for the visit (molars can take longer).

After the root canal, the tooth often needs a crown for long-term strength. We will discuss timing at your visit.
