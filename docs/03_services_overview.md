# Services Overview

BrightSmile offers the following services:

- **Preventive care:** exams, cleanings, fluoride, sealants, oral cancer screening
- **Restorative:** fillings (composite), crowns, bridges, inlays/onlays
- **Endodontics:** root canal therapy on most teeth (complex molars may be referred)
- **Periodontics:** deep cleaning (scaling and root planing), periodontal maintenance
- **Oral surgery:** simple extractions, surgical extractions, wisdom teeth (select cases)
- **Implants:** single-tooth implants and implant-supported crowns (consult required)
- **Cosmetic:** teeth whitening (in-office and take-home), bonding, veneers
- **Pediatric:** care for children age 3+, including first-visit education
- **Emergency:** same-day triage for pain, broken teeth, and lost fillings when slots allow

We do not currently offer orthodontics (braces/Invisalign) in-house; we can refer you to trusted local orthodontists.
