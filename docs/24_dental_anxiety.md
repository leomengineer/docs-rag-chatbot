# Dental Anxiety and Comfort Options

Many patients feel nervous about dental visits. Tell us when you book or at check-in — we can adjust the pace, explain each step, and use comfort measures when appropriate.

## What we offer

**Nitrous oxide (laughing gas)**  
Available for restorative work, extractions, and other procedures. You stay awake and can usually drive yourself home after a short recovery period in the chair. Not typically used for routine cleanings.

**Topical and local anesthesia**  
We use numbing gel before injections and check in during treatment so you are not in pain.

**Breaks and signals**  
Raise your hand anytime for a pause. We can split longer treatments into shorter visits when possible.

**Headphones and distraction**  
Bring your own earbuds or ask the front desk — many rooms have screens for short visits.

## Before your visit

- Share past bad experiences or specific triggers (gagging, needle anxiety, sounds).
- Ask about nitrous oxide when scheduling if you think you will need it.
- For severe anxiety or complex treatment, we can discuss referral to a sedation dentist in our network.

## After your visit

Mild soreness after numbing wears off is normal. Call (951) 555-0142 if you have unusual pain, swelling, or questions about recovery.
