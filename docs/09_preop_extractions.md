# Pre-Op Instructions: Extractions

Follow these instructions before your extraction appointment:

1. Eat a light meal unless you were told to fast (sedation cases only).
2. Take your usual medications unless the dentist told you to pause blood thinners — never stop blood thinners without clearance from the prescribing doctor.
3. Arrange a ride home if you will receive oral sedation or IV sedation.
4. Wear comfortable clothing; avoid contact lenses if sedation is planned.
5. Tell us about allergies, pregnancy, or recent illness when you check in.

If you feel sick the morning of surgery (fever, vomiting, productive cough), call us — we may reschedule for safety.
