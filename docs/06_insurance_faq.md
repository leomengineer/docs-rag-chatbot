# Insurance FAQ

**Do you accept my insurance?**  
We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna PPO, MetLife PDP, and Guardian. We also file claims as out-of-network for many other plans. Call the front desk with your member ID and we will verify benefits before your visit.

**Do you accept Medicaid / Medi-Cal dental?**  
Not at this time.

**What is my copay?**  
It depends on your plan and the procedure. Preventive visits (exam + cleaning) are often covered at 100% in-network. Fillings and crowns usually have a patient portion. We provide an estimate after verifying benefits.

**When do I pay?**  
Patient portions are due at the time of service unless a payment plan is arranged in advance.

**Will you bill my medical insurance?**  
Generally no. Dental procedures are billed to dental insurance. Rare exceptions (trauma, some oral surgery) may involve medical billing — ask at consultation.
