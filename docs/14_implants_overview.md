# Dental Implants Overview

A dental implant is a titanium post placed in the jawbone to support a crown, bridge, or denture.

**Typical timeline**

1. Consult + 3D imaging (CBCT)
2. Implant placement surgery
3. Healing / osseointegration: usually 3–6 months
4. Abutment and final crown

**Am I a candidate?**  
Adequate bone, healthy gums, and good general health. Smokers have higher failure risk and must be counseled. Uncontrolled diabetes or recent IV bisphosphonate use may require medical clearance.

**Cost:**  
A single implant + abutment + crown typically ranges from $3,500 to $5,500 cash, depending on bone grafting needs. Insurance coverage varies widely; many plans cover little or none of the implant fixture.
