# Periodontal (Gum) Therapy

**What is scaling and root planing (deep cleaning)?**  
A deeper cleaning below the gumline to remove tartar and bacteria from periodontal pockets. Often done by quadrant under local anesthetic, sometimes over two visits.

**After deep cleaning**

- Expect sore gums for a few days; soft foods help.
- Rinse with prescribed antimicrobial rinse if provided.
- Resume gentle brushing the same day; floss carefully.

**Maintenance:**  
After active therapy, periodontal maintenance visits every 3–4 months are recommended instead of standard 6-month cleanings. Skipping maintenance is the most common reason disease returns.
