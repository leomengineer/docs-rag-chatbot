# Emergency Care Policy

Dental emergencies include severe toothache, swelling, trauma, a knocked-out tooth, or uncontrolled bleeding after a procedure.

**During office hours:** Call (951) 555-0142. We reserve same-day emergency slots when possible.

**After hours:** Call the same number and follow the prompt for the on-call dentist. After-hours advice is for urgent triage; not all issues require an immediate visit.

**Knocked-out permanent tooth:** Keep the tooth moist (milk or saliva), do not scrub the root, and seek care within 60 minutes if possible.

**When to go to the ER:** Difficulty breathing or swallowing, facial trauma with possible fracture, or uncontrolled bleeding that does not stop with pressure. The ER can manage medical emergencies; definitive dental repair usually happens at a dental office afterward.
