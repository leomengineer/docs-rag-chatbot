# Night Guards and Teeth Grinding (Bruxism)

BrightSmile Dental Clinic offers custom night guards for patients who grind or clench their teeth at night.

## Who needs a night guard?

- Morning jaw pain or headaches
- Worn, flattened, or chipped teeth
- Partner reports grinding sounds during sleep
- History of TMJ discomfort

## What we offer

A custom acrylic night guard is molded from an impression or digital scan of your teeth. Soft boil-and-bite store guards are not recommended for long-term use — they can worsen bite problems.

## Visits and timeline

1. **Scan / impression** — usually done at a regular checkup or a short dedicated visit.
2. **Delivery** — the lab-made guard is ready in about **10–14 days**. We check the fit and adjust as needed.

Insurance sometimes covers part of a night guard when grinding has caused documented tooth wear. We verify benefits before treatment.

## Care tips

- Rinse with cool water after each use; brush gently with a soft toothbrush.
- Store in the ventilated case we provide — do not leave it in hot cars or boiling water.
- Bring the guard to your cleaning visits so we can check wear.
