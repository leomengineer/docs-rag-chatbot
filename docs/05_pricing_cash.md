# Pricing Guide (Cash / Uninsured)

Prices below are approximate cash rates before any discounts. Exact fees depend on clinical findings. A written estimate is provided before treatment.

| Service | Typical cash range |
|---------|-------------------|
| New patient exam + X-rays | $150 – $220 |
| Adult cleaning | $120 – $160 |
| Child cleaning | $90 – $130 |
| Composite filling (1 surface) | $180 – $250 |
| Crown (porcelain) | $1,100 – $1,400 |
| Root canal (front tooth) | $800 – $1,100 |
| Root canal (molar) | $1,200 – $1,600 |
| Simple extraction | $200 – $350 |
| Surgical extraction | $350 – $650 |
| In-office whitening | $450 – $550 |
| Take-home whitening kit | $250 – $320 |

We offer a 5% courtesy discount when the full balance is paid on the day of treatment (cash or card). Financing through CareCredit is available for treatment plans over $500.
