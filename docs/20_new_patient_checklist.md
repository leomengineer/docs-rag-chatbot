# New Patient Checklist

Before your first visit, please:

- [ ] Complete online health history forms (link sent by email/text)
- [ ] Bring photo ID and dental insurance card
- [ ] List all medications and allergies
- [ ] Note any dental anxiety or past bad experiences so we can plan comfort measures
- [ ] Arrive 15 minutes early

If you have recent X-rays from another office (within 12 months), ask them to email us — we may be able to avoid retaking some images.
