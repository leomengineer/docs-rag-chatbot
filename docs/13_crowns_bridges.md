# Crowns and Bridges

**When is a crown needed?**  
Large fillings, cracked teeth, after root canal therapy, or for cosmetic reshaping.

**How many visits?**  
Usually two: (1) prepare the tooth, take impressions or digital scans, place a temporary crown; (2) cement the permanent crown 2–3 weeks later. Same-day crowns are available for select cases using our in-office mill.

**Bridge vs implant:**  
A bridge uses neighboring teeth as anchors. An implant replaces the root without preparing adjacent teeth. We discuss pros, timeline, and cost at consultation.

**Caring for a temporary crown:**  
Avoid sticky foods (caramel, gum) and floss by pulling the floss out to the side rather than up, so you do not dislodge the temporary.
