# Pediatric Visits

We welcome children age 3 and older.

**First visit tips**

- Schedule a morning appointment when your child is rested.
- Avoid promising “no shots” — we always tell the truth in age-appropriate language.
- Bring a comfort item if helpful.

**Fluoride and sealants**  
Fluoride varnish is often applied after cleanings. Sealants on permanent molars help prevent cavities in the grooves and are typically recommended around ages 6 and 12 when those teeth erupt.

**Behavior guidance**  
We use tell-show-do and positive reinforcement. For highly anxious children or extensive treatment needs, we may refer to a pediatric dentist who offers additional sedation options.
